Average Calculator guide

Mean formula

Add all values and divide by the number of values.

Count matters

The denominator is how many numbers are in the list.

Other averages

Median and mode are different measures and may give different answers.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate the mean?

Add the values together and divide by how many values there are.

Can I enter decimals?

Yes. Separate decimal values with commas or spaces.

Is mean the same as median?

No. The median is the middle value after sorting; the mean uses the total divided by the count.
