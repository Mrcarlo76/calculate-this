Daily macro split—
Enter calories and weight to calculate.

This is a planning tool, not a personalised diet prescription. The protein factor and fat percentage are explicit choices; carbohydrates receive the remaining calories. 1 g protein/carbohydrate = 4 kcal and 1 g fat = 9 kcal.

How it works

Protein

Body weight is multiplied by your selected grams-per-kilogram planning factor.

Fat

Your selected percentage of calories is divided by 9 to estimate fat grams.

Carbohydrate

Calories left after protein and fat are divided by 4 to estimate carbohydrate grams.
