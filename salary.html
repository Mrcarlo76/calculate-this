Frequently asked questions

Is this calculator for the UK?

Yes. It is designed around UK Income Tax and employee National Insurance assumptions for the tax year shown.

Does it include pension deductions?

Not unless the calculator explicitly includes them in the inputs.

Will my payslip match exactly?

Not always. Tax codes, benefits, bonuses and other payroll deductions can change the final amount.
