Calculator updates

A visible record of important rate checks and calculator changes.

13 August 2026 — v3.5.1

Internal linking & discovery: added curated Related calculators sections across 96 calculator pages using crawlable HTML links and descriptive calculator names. Priority journeys connect closely related tools such as Mortgage → Stamp Duty/LTV, Metal Stud → Plasterboard/Insulation/MF Ceiling, and BMI → Calorie/Macro/Protein/Water.

13 August 2026 — v3.5.1

Financial trust pass: VAT now shows current UK VAT rates, a worked example and GOV.UK sources. Mortgage and Loan calculators now show ±1 percentage-point repayment sensitivity and clearer assumptions. Compound Interest now shows ±2 percentage-point growth scenarios and explicitly states the compounding, contribution timing and exclusions behind the projection.

13 August 2026 — v3.5.1

Construction accuracy pass: Plasterboard now supports one or two layers per face and 3000 × 600 boards; Insulation separates net and ordering area; Screed and Concrete clearly separate net volume from ordering allowance; Brick & Block now allows significant openings to be deducted before waste is applied. Printable assumptions were updated to match.

13 August 2026 — v3.5.1

Metal Stud Partition Calculator: every single or double door opening now adds one additional full-height whipper/support stud to the stud order. The extra stud is also included when comparing the most efficient stud stock length.

13 August 2026

MF Ceiling Calculator: waste allowance now carries through to MF5, MF6 and MF7. MF7 take-off now uses explicit channel runs at maximum 1200mm centres and includes the edge run.

Ceiling Tile Calculator: 600 × 600 grid now reports 1200mm and 600mm cross tees separately; 1200 × 600 tiles use 1200mm cross tees only.

12 August 2026

Salary Calculator: 2026/27 Income Tax, Scottish Income Tax and employee Class 1 NI rates checked against HMRC/GOV.UK. Added visible rate tables, sources and a worked example.

Stamp Duty Calculator: residential SDLT rates checked against HMRC/GOV.UK. Added the 2% non-UK-resident surcharge option, visible rate table, sources and a worked example.

How updates work

Rule-dependent calculators are reviewed when a new tax year, published rate change or material rule change affects their result. The relevant calculator page shows when its rates were last checked and links to the official source used.
