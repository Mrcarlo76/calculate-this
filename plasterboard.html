How to estimate plasterboard quantities

Enter the surface dimensions, board size and allowance required. The calculator converts the area into the number of full boards needed and includes the selected waste allowance so the result is practical for ordering.

Measure the wall or ceiling area carefully and allow for cuts, damaged boards and layout. Actual quantities can vary around openings and complicated details.

What is included in the board quantity

The calculator multiplies the measured surface by the selected number of boarded faces and layers per face, applies the waste allowance, divides by the chosen sheet area and rounds up to full boards. Openings are not automatically deducted because board layout and reusable offcuts can make a simple area deduction misleading.
