Frequently asked questions

How do I convert hourly pay to annual salary?

Multiply hourly rate by weekly hours and then by paid weeks per year.

Why does the calculator ask for paid weeks?

Not everyone is paid for all 52 weeks, so this makes the estimate more flexible.

Is this take-home pay?

No. It is gross pay before deductions.
