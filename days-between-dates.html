Frequently asked questions

Does the calculator include leap years?

Yes. Leap days are included automatically.

What counts as a working day?

Working-day mode treats Monday to Friday as working days and excludes official bank holidays for the selected UK region.

Do bank holidays differ around the UK?

Yes. Choose England & Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland so the correct official bank holidays are excluded.

How are weeks handled?

In calendar mode, one week equals 7 calendar days. In working-day mode, one week equals 5 working days, with weekends and official bank holidays still excluded.
