Days between dates guide

Choose any two dates to calculate the elapsed time between them. The tool gives the exact number of days and useful equivalent figures such as weeks and hours.

Exact days

The day count uses the actual calendar difference between the two selected dates.

Useful equivalents

Weeks and hours can make longer or shorter time spans easier to understand.

Any date range

Use it for deadlines, holidays, project planning, anniversaries or historical dates.

Frequently asked questions

Does the calculator include leap years?

Yes. Leap days are included automatically when they fall inside the selected range.

Can I compare dates in either order?

Yes. The tool can calculate the distance between two dates regardless of which one you enter first.

Are months exact?

Month equivalents are approximate because calendar months have different lengths.
