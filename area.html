Area Calculator guide

Rectangle

Area = length × width.

Circle

Area = π × radius². Enter the diameter and the calculator halves it to get the radius.

Unit conversion

Dimensions are converted to metres internally before the area is calculated.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the area of a rectangle?

Multiply its length by its width.

How do I find the area of a circle?

Multiply π by the radius squared.

Why are square units different from normal units?

Area measures two dimensions, so the unit is squared, such as m² or ft².
