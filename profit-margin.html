Margin explained

Gross profit

Selling price minus cost price.

Profit margin

Gross profit divided by selling price, expressed as a percentage.

Margin vs markup

Margin uses selling price as the denominator; markup uses cost price.

Frequently asked questions

What is the profit margin formula?

Profit margin = profit ÷ selling price × 100.

Is margin the same as markup?

No. Markup measures profit relative to cost, while margin measures profit relative to selling price.

Can margin be negative?

Yes. If the selling price is below cost, the margin is negative.
