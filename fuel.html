Work out the petrol or diesel cost of a journey from distance, UK MPG and pump price per litre. The calculator is useful for trip costs, commuting, mileage planning and comparing how much different vehicles cost to run.

Fuel price

Worked example: petrol cost for a journey

For a 150-mile journey in a car returning 45 UK MPG with petrol at 145p per litre, the calculator converts imperial gallons to litres, works out the fuel used and multiplies it by the pump price. Change the number of journeys to price a return trip or repeated commute.

Formula: miles ÷ UK MPG × 4.54609 litres per imperial gallon × price per litre.

Frequently asked questions

Does this use UK MPG?

Yes. The calculator is designed around imperial gallons used for UK MPG figures.

Can I calculate a return journey?

Yes. Either double the distance or increase the number of journeys.

Why is my real fuel cost different?

Driving speed, traffic, temperature, tyres and vehicle load can all affect real fuel economy.
