Paint Calculator guide

Coverage

Paint tins usually state approximate square metres covered per litre.

Multiple coats

Two coats roughly doubles the paintable area the product needs to cover.

Allowance

A small extra percentage helps cover touch-ups, texture and application losses.

Frequently asked questions

Should I subtract windows and doors?

For a closer estimate, use the paintable wall area after subtracting large openings.

How many coats should I use?

That depends on the paint, colour change and surface. Use the number recommended for your project.

Is paint coverage exact?

No. Surface texture, application method and product can change real coverage.
