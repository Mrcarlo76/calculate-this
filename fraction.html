Fraction Simplifier guide

Lowest terms

Divide numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor.

Denominator

The denominator cannot be zero.

Decimal form

Divide the numerator by the denominator to convert the fraction to a decimal.

Frequently asked questions

How do I simplify 24/36?

The greatest common divisor is 12, so the simplified fraction is 2/3.

Can the denominator be zero?

No. Division by zero is undefined.

Can the fraction be negative?

Yes. The calculator keeps the negative sign with the numerator.
