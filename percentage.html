How to calculate a percentage

To find a percentage of a number, divide the percentage by 100 and multiply by the number. For example, 20% of 150 is 30 because 20 ÷ 100 × 150 = 30.

Increase or decrease by a percentage

For an increase, multiply the starting number by 1 plus the percentage as a decimal. For a decrease, multiply by 1 minus the percentage as a decimal. Use the tabs above and the calculator does the working for you.

Percentage calculator guide

Percentages are a quick way to describe a part of a whole as an amount out of 100. This calculator handles three common percentage calculations: finding a percentage of a number, increasing a number by a percentage, and decreasing a number by a percentage.

Find X% of Y

Formula: (X ÷ 100) × Y

Example: 15% of 80 = 12.

Increase by X%

Formula: Y × (1 + X ÷ 100)

Example: 80 increased by 15% = 92.

Decrease by X%

Formula: Y × (1 − X ÷ 100)

Example: 80 decreased by 15% = 68.

Percentage calculator FAQs

How do I work out 20% of a number?

Multiply the number by 0.20. For example, 20% of 250 is 50.

How do I add a percentage to a number?

Convert the percentage to a decimal, add 1, then multiply by the starting number. To add 10%, multiply by 1.10.

How do I subtract a percentage from a number?

Convert the percentage to a decimal and subtract it from 1. To reduce a number by 25%, multiply it by 0.75.

Is a percentage decrease the same as percentage change?

Not quite. Decreasing a number by a chosen percentage gives a new value. Percentage change measures the relative difference between an original value and a new value.
