VAT calculator guide

Add VAT to a net price or remove VAT from a gross price. The calculator separates the underlying amount, VAT element and final total.

Add VAT

Multiply the net amount by 1 plus the VAT rate as a decimal.

Remove VAT

Divide the gross amount by 1 plus the VAT rate to find the net amount.

VAT element

The VAT amount is the difference between the gross and net figures.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add 20% VAT?

Multiply the net amount by 1.20.

How do I remove 20% VAT?

Divide the VAT-inclusive total by 1.20.

Is VAT always 20% in the UK?

No. Different goods and services can have different VAT treatments, so use the appropriate rate for your situation.
