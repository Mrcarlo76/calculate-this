How to calculate screed volume

Screed volume is based on floor area multiplied by screed thickness. The calculator converts the entered dimensions into a practical material volume and allows for the assumptions shown in the tool.

Check the specified minimum thickness and allow for uneven substrates, falls and site waste before ordering.
