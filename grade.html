Grade Calculator guide

Weighted average

Multiply each score by its weight, add the results and divide by the total weight.

Weights

Larger weights give an assessment more influence on the final result.

Normalisation

If the entered weights do not total 100%, the calculator normalises across what you entered.

Frequently asked questions

Do my weights have to total 100%?

No, but 100% is easiest to interpret. Other totals are normalised across the entered assessments.

Can I leave unused rows blank?

Yes.

Is this the same as a simple average?

Only when every assessment has the same weight.
