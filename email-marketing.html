Email privacy features and client behaviour can make open-rate data less reliable than clicks and conversions. Treat opens as one signal rather than an exact measure of readership.

How email marketing metrics work

What it measures

This tool turns delivered emails, opens, clicks, conversions and revenue into comparable campaign rates.

Formula

Open rate = opens ÷ delivered; CTR = clicks ÷ delivered; conversion rate = conversions ÷ delivered.

Use the result

Compare the metric over time or between campaigns using the same measurement basis.
