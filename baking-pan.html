How it works

Compares the surface area of the original and new tins. Multiply every ingredient quantity by the resulting factor. Similar pan depths are important because very different depths can change baking time and heat transfer.

How baking pan conversion works

Different pan shapes and sizes change the available baking area or volume. The calculator compares the original and replacement pans so you can scale a recipe more sensibly.

Baking depth and cooking time can still change even when pan capacity is similar, so use the conversion as a guide and monitor the bake.
