Percentage change calculator guide

Percentage change shows how much a value has risen or fallen relative to its original value. It is useful for prices, performance, statistics and business figures.

Percentage increase

A positive result means the new value is higher than the original value.

Percentage decrease

A negative result means the new value is lower than the original value.

Original value matters

Percentage change is measured relative to the starting value, not simply the difference between the two numbers.

Frequently asked questions

What is the percentage change formula?

Percentage change = (new value − original value) ÷ original value × 100.

Can percentage change be over 100%?

Yes. If the increase is larger than the original value, the percentage increase can exceed 100%.

Why can a decrease not go below −100%?

A value cannot fall by more than its entire original amount unless it becomes negative.
