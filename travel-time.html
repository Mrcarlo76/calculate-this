Travel Time Calculator guide

Core formula

Time = distance ÷ average speed.

Average speed

Real journeys include traffic, junctions and stops, so your moving speed may differ from overall average speed.

Planning buffer

For appointments or flights, add extra time for delays rather than relying on the mathematical minimum.

Frequently asked questions

Does this include traffic?

No. It uses the average speed you enter.

Can I use kilometres?

Yes. Select kilometres and km/h.

Why does a real trip often take longer?

Stops, congestion and variable speeds reduce the overall average speed.
