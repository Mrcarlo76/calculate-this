Metal stud wall & partition calculator

Use the wall length, height and stud centres to estimate framing and boards to order, including stock lengths and splice allowances.

For spliced uprights, effective assembled height is total stud material minus 600 mm for each splice. Base track remains the full partition length. Head track adds 1.30 m per single door and 2.10 m per double door. Board quantities can be calculated for one or two layers each side. The stud recommendation compares 3.6 m, 4.2 m, 4.8 m, 5.0 m and 6.0 m stock lengths and minimises total extra stud material, so splice overlap is counted as inefficiency as well as cutting offcut.

Metal stud wall take-off

Unlike a simple metal-frame area calculator, this take-off considers available stud lengths, 600mm splice overlaps for tall walls, separate head and base track, door-head track and one additional whipper/support stud for each door opening.

This is a quantity take-off rather than a cost calculator. Check the project specification and manufacturer system details before ordering.

Built around practical drylining take-off logic

This tool goes beyond a simple wall-area calculation: it models stud centres, available stock lengths, 600mm splice overlaps, separate base and head track, door-head track, whipper/support studs and board layers. The ordering result is deliberately rounded to purchasable stock rather than presenting an unrealistically precise theoretical quantity.
