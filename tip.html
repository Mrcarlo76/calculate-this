Frequently asked questions

How much is a 10% tip?

It is one tenth of the bill. On a £60 bill, 10% is £6.

Can I split the bill between several people?

Yes. Enter the number of people and the calculator divides the total evenly.

Does everyone have to tip the same amount?

This calculator assumes an even split. Individual shares would need to be adjusted separately.
