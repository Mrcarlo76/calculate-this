CPM Calculator

Calculate advertising cost per 1,000 impressions from campaign spend and impressions delivered.

Your result—

CPM measures media cost rather than clicks, conversions or profit. Use it with outcome metrics when evaluating campaigns.

How CPM works

What it measures

CPM standardises impression-based advertising costs to the price paid for 1,000 impressions.

Formula

CPM = ad spend ÷ impressions × 1,000.

Use the result

Compare the metric over time or between campaigns using the same measurement basis.
