Frequently asked questions

Does this assume the rate stays the same?

Yes. It is a projection based on the rate entered remaining constant.

Can I use it for investments?

You can model a hypothetical return, but investments are not guaranteed and can lose value.

Why do regular deposits matter?

They increase the amount that can earn interest or returns over time.
