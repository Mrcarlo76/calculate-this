Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between pace and speed?

Pace is time per unit of distance; speed is distance covered per unit of time.

Can I use this for walking or cycling?

Yes, although pace per kilometre or mile is most commonly used for running and walking.

Does it account for hills or stops?

No. It calculates average pace from total distance and total elapsed time.
