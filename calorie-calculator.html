Calorie calculator guide

This calculator first estimates basal metabolic rate (BMR), then applies an activity multiplier to estimate total daily energy expenditure (TDEE). The result is a practical starting point for maintenance calories.

1. Estimate BMR

The Mifflin–St Jeor equation uses weight, height, age and sex to estimate energy use at rest.

2. Apply activity

BMR is multiplied by the activity factor you select to estimate average daily energy expenditure.

3. Set a target

The displayed lose and gain targets use modest 10% adjustments around estimated maintenance rather than extreme deficits or surpluses.

Frequently asked questions

What are maintenance calories?

Maintenance calories are an estimate of the daily energy intake that would keep body weight broadly stable at the selected activity level.

How are daily calorie needs estimated?

This calculator estimates BMR with the Mifflin–St Jeor equation and multiplies it by a standard activity factor to estimate total daily energy expenditure.

Are calorie targets exact?

No. They are starting estimates. Real energy needs vary, so compare the estimate with your weight trend, performance and hunger over time.
