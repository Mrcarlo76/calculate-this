How it works

Tiles

Room area is divided by tile area, then the chosen waste percentage is added.

Main tees

A simple planning estimate assumes main tees at roughly 1200 mm centres.

Cross tees

600 × 600 tiles require both 1200mm cross tees and 600mm cross tees. 1200 × 600 tiles use the 1200mm cross tees without the intermediate 600mm tees.

Perimeter

Perimeter trim is based on the full room perimeter.

How the ceiling grid is estimated

The grid estimate separates main tees, 1200mm cross tees and 600mm cross tees. For 600 × 600 tiles the intermediate 600mm tee divides each 1200 × 600 module; 1200 × 600 tiles do not need that intermediate tee. Actual perimeter cuts and the chosen manufacturer's grid layout can alter quantities.
