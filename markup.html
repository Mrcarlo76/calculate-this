Markup explained

Markup formula

Selling price = cost × (1 + markup percentage).

Markup amount

The cash markup is the difference between selling price and cost.

Not the same as margin

A 50% markup does not mean a 50% profit margin.

Frequently asked questions

What is a 50% markup on £60?

The markup is £30, giving a selling price of £90.

Why is markup different from margin?

Markup is measured against cost; margin is measured against selling price.

Can I use this before VAT?

Yes. Businesses often calculate a net selling price first and then apply VAT where required.
