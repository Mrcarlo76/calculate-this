Mifflin–St Jeor

Frequently asked questions

Is BMR the same as daily calorie needs?

No. Total daily energy use also includes movement, exercise and the energy cost of digestion.

Is this a medical measurement?

No. It is an estimate from a predictive equation, not a direct metabolic test.

Why does the equation ask for sex?

The published Mifflin–St Jeor equations use different constants for male and female subjects.
