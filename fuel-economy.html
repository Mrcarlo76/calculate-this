Fuel Economy Converter guide

UK vs US gallons

An imperial gallon is larger than a US gallon, so the MPG figures are not directly interchangeable.

L/100 km

Lower litres per 100 km means better fuel economy.

MPG

Frequently asked questions

Is UK MPG the same as US MPG?

No. The gallons are different sizes, so the numeric MPG values differ.

Is lower L/100 km better?

Yes. It means fewer litres are used to travel 100 km.

Can I convert from any of the three units?

Yes. Choose the unit you already have and the calculator shows all three.
