Frequently asked questions

What is compound interest?

It is growth earned on both the original amount and previously accumulated growth.

Why does time matter so much?

Compounding has more opportunities to build on itself over longer periods.

Are investment returns guaranteed?

No. Investment returns can rise or fall, so projections are estimates only.
