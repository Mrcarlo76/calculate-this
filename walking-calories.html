MET calculations are population-level estimates and can differ from an individual's actual energy use. Incline, terrain, fitness, age, gait and weather can all change the result.

How walking calories are estimated

The calculator uses the standard MET calorie equation: MET × 3.5 × body weight (kg) ÷ 200 × minutes. Pace options use walking MET values from the 2024 Adult Compendium of Physical Activities.
