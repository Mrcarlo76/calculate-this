Loan-to-value calculator guide

Loan-to-value compares the size of a mortgage or secured loan with the property value. It is one of the key figures lenders use when assessing property borrowing.

LTV formula

LTV = mortgage balance ÷ property value × 100.

Equity

Property equity is the property value minus the outstanding mortgage.

Lower LTV

A lower LTV generally means you own a larger share of the property outright.

Frequently asked questions

What does 80% LTV mean?

It means the mortgage is equal to 80% of the property value and the remaining 20% is equity or deposit.

Is lower LTV usually better?

Lower LTV can reduce lender risk and may give access to a wider range of mortgage products.

Can LTV change over time?

Yes. It changes as the mortgage balance falls or the property value moves.
