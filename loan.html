Loan repayment calculator guide

Use the loan amount, annual interest rate and repayment term to estimate the monthly repayment, total amount repaid and total interest.

Monthly repayment

For a standard repayment loan, each monthly payment covers interest and reduces the outstanding balance.

Total interest

Longer terms can lower the monthly payment but often increase the total interest paid.

APR and rate

Use the annual borrowing rate shown for the loan. Fees may mean the advertised APR differs from the simple interest rate.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if the interest rate is 0%?

The calculator divides the loan amount evenly across the repayment months.

Does a longer loan term reduce monthly payments?

Usually yes, but it can increase the total interest paid over the life of the loan.

Does this include loan fees?

Only if those fees are included in the amount or rate you enter.
