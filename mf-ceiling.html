How the MF ceiling estimate works

The calculator lays out MF5 ceiling sections at 400 mm centres and MF7 primary support channels at a maximum 1200 mm centres, includes the edge runs, and applies the selected waste allowance to MF5, MF6 and MF7 ordering quantities.

Perimeter details, hangers, bulkheads, changes in level and manufacturer-specific systems can affect the final take-off.

What the MF take-off is doing

MF5 and MF7 are laid out as channel runs rather than estimated from area alone, including edge runs before the selected waste allowance is applied. MF6 follows the ceiling perimeter. The result is a planning take-off and should still be checked against the project ceiling specification, loading and manufacturer system detail.
