BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis. It does not account for factors such as muscle mass, pregnancy, age or ethnicity.

BMI calculator guide

BMI is a simple screening measure based on weight relative to height. Enter metric or imperial measurements to calculate your BMI and see the standard adult BMI category.

Metric formula

BMI = weight in kilograms ÷ height in metres squared.

Imperial formula

BMI can also be calculated from pounds and inches using the standard conversion factor.

Use as a screening tool

BMI does not directly measure body fat and can be less informative for some people, including very muscular individuals.

Frequently asked questions

What is a healthy BMI range?

For most adults, 18.5 to 24.9 is commonly described as the healthy range.

Is BMI a diagnosis?

No. BMI is a screening measure and should not be treated as a medical diagnosis.

Can athletes have a high BMI?

Yes. Higher muscle mass can raise BMI even when body fat is relatively low.
