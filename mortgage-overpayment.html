How overpayments can help

Reduce the balance faster

Extra payments reduce the amount on which future interest is charged.

Shorten the term

Keeping the normal payment plus an overpayment can repay the mortgage earlier.

Check lender rules

Some mortgages limit penalty-free overpayments or charge early repayment fees.

Frequently asked questions

Does this assume the rate stays fixed?

Yes. The estimate assumes the entered interest rate remains unchanged.

Are overpayments always penalty-free?

No. Check your mortgage terms for allowances and early repayment charges.

Does this change my required monthly payment?

The model keeps the scheduled payment and adds the chosen overpayment.
