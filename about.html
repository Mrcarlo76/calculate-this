About Calculate This

Calculate This is a growing collection of clear, practical calculators and everyday tools.

What we’re building

The aim is simple: useful tools that are quick to understand, pleasant to use and free from unnecessary friction. The site covers everyday subjects including money, home, travel, health, education, business and text.

What the calculators are for

Most tools are designed for estimates, planning, learning or quick checks. Where a result can depend on official rules, personal circumstances or professional judgement, the page should say so clearly.

Corrections and improvements

Calculators are tested as the site develops, but no website is infallible. If a rule, formula or assumption changes, the relevant tool should be reviewed and updated rather than quietly treated as timeless.
