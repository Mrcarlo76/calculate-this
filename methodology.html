How we calculate

A plain-English explanation of how Calculate This approaches formulas, assumptions and calculator quality.

Formulas first

Each calculator is built around an explicit formula or defined calculation method. Results are generated in the browser from the values you enter; we avoid hiding the basic logic behind vague scores or unexplained outputs.

Assumptions matter

Some calculations require assumptions. Mortgage projections can assume a constant interest rate, paint estimates depend on coverage, and health equations are estimates rather than direct measurements. Relevant assumptions are explained on the calculator page.

Rules that can change

Calculators affected by tax bands, government rules or other changing thresholds need extra care. Those tools should be checked against an appropriate primary source when they are created or materially updated.

Rounding

Calculations may use more precision internally than is displayed. Results are rounded to a sensible number of decimal places or currency units so they remain readable.

Not professional advice

Calculate This provides informational tools, not financial, tax, legal or medical advice. For decisions where accuracy has significant consequences, verify the result against the relevant official guidance or a qualified professional.

Our review process

Each calculator starts with a defined formula or take-off method. We test representative inputs and edge cases, keep rounding explicit, and distinguish theoretical quantities from quantities that must be ordered as full boards, packs or stock lengths.

For calculators that depend on changing external rules or rates, we prefer primary sources such as GOV.UK or HMRC and show a last-checked date on the calculator where that date materially affects trust in the result. A change is recorded on the Updates page when it alters a rule, assumption or calculation.

We do not change dates merely to make pages appear fresh. A review date is intended to mean the calculation or its underlying assumptions were actually checked.
