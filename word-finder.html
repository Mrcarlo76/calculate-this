How the Word Finder works

The search checks a local English word list in your browser, so results appear without sending your letters to a server. Repeated letters are respected: for example, a word needing two E's will only appear if your letter set contains at least two E's.

Word list attribution is included with the site in data/WORDLIST-LICENSE.txt.

Word Finder guide

Enter your available letters and use the filters to find words that can be made without using any letter more times than it appears in your set.

Repeated letters

A word that needs two of the same letter only appears if your input contains that letter at least twice.

Length filters

Set minimum and maximum lengths to narrow down the results.

Extra filters

Use starts-with and contains filters when you know part of the word you are looking for.

Frequently asked questions

Can the Word Finder use a letter more than once?

Only when that letter appears more than once in the letters you entered.

Can I filter by word length?

Yes. Use the minimum and maximum length controls.

Are my letters sent to a server?

No. The current tool checks a local word list in your browser.
