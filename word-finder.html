Word Finder

Enter a set of letters and instantly find words you can make from them. Each letter can only be used as many times as it appears in your letters.

How the Word Finder works

The search checks a local English word list in your browser, so results appear without sending your letters to a server. Repeated letters are respected: for example, a word needing two E's will only appear if your letter set contains at least two E's.

Word list attribution is included with the site in data/WORDLIST-LICENSE.txt.
