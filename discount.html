Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate 25% off?

Multiply the original price by 0.75, or enter 25% into the calculator.

Is 50% off the same as half price?

Yes. A 50% discount leaves 50% of the original price.

Can I stack two discounts by adding them together?

Not usually. Two sequential discounts are applied one after the other, so 20% off then 10% off is not the same as 30% off.
